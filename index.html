About

Meet Madhuri.

A counsellor and PhD Scholar in Psychology, with a deep commitment to supporting healthier, more meaningful lives.

Founder, Earthitude Research Forum
Behavioural and Environmental Research

Madhuri works with individuals, students, families, couples, partners, working professionals and communities as they navigate emotional and psychological challenges.

Her approach is founded on compassion, empathy, ethical practice and complete confidentiality. Through evidence-based counselling, she helps people build emotional resilience, improve relationships, manage stress and meet life's challenges with confidence.

Alongside traditional mental health counselling, Madhuri specialises in Climate Change Counselling and Sustainability Psychology—supporting understanding of climate anxiety, eco-grief, environmental stress and behavioural change for sustainable living.

Currently a PhD Scholar in Psychology; PhD expected to be awarded by December 2026.
